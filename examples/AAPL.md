## Overview

## Recent financials
For the fiscal year 2025, Apple reported total revenue of $416.2 billion, up from $391.0 billion in the prior year [income_statement 2025-09-30]. This represents a year-over-year revenue growth of 6.4%. Gross profit was $195.2 billion, and net income attributable to common stockholders was $112.0 billion, a significant increase from $93.7 billion in fiscal 2024 [income_statement 2025-09-30]. The company generated $111.5 billion in operating cash flow [cashflow 2025-09-30].
